# Code of Conduct

Flight adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to everyone taking part in this project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

We want Flight to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, own your mistakes, and focus on what's best for the community.

**Don't:** use sexualised language or imagery, troll, make insulting or derogatory comments or personal or political attacks, harass anyone publicly or privately, publish others' private information without permission, or engage in any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@akhanrepo](https://github.com/akhanrepo)). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
